How to play (demo v1.4)

On foot: left thumbstick to walk — push gently to stroll, full push to run. Drag right side to look.
Driving: left ◀ ▶ buttons steer, right pedals = gas / brake / handbrake.
Action button (teal): ENTER/EXIT the Safari van, ENTER/EXIT house at the doors, PICK UP items.
Inventory: backpack button (right of the minimap) or press I — tap DROP to put an item back down.
Minimap (top-left): tap to open the full map (opens on you, zoomed in) — pinch to zoom, drag to pan, tap to close.
Goal: check the world, streets, controls and the van.
