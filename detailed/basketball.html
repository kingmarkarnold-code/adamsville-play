🏀 Basketball

The backyard court at 535 Dollar Mill Road, from Surviving Adamsville.

In the main game, walk in between the trees behind the house at 535 and the court will invite you to play.

Controls — move with the joystick, the WASD / arrow keys, or a gamepad stick. Pick up the ball with the button below, Space, or gamepad A — and HOLD that same button to charge your shot: the longer you hold, the harder it goes, and letting go at the perfect moment is what makes it. Release too soon and it falls short; hold too long and it goes too hard. When HE has the ball, use the Defend and Steal buttons below (keys F and G, gamepad Y and X). Gamepad B asks to leave. A Bluetooth keyboard or controller works the same way.
