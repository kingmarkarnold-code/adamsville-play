The nine gestures

Tap a card, double-tap it, or press-and-hold it — or swipe it with one finger (four directions) or two fingers (up / down). On the laptop: click, double-click, click-and-hold, drag with the mouse or trackpad, or use the keys. What each gesture means is declared by the queue you are reviewing — where a queue declares no meaning for a gesture, the deck records the gesture only.
