Tatum Lake — falls & culvert TEST▾

A test scene, same treatment as the crest one — package terrain, real individual trees, a look-test road — framed on Tatum Lake, its outlet, and where the outlet creek crosses Boulder Park Dr. Test scene only, not game data; no package data was changed.

The falls: where the lake (822½ ft) meets its creek, the water comes over a lip of land and boulders — 44 assorted stones, about 0.6–2½ m across, grey-brown, clustered across the channel with some standing in the flow. The measured step down is 6.0 ft, so the boulders are worked as a stepped cascade: three small drops of about 2 ft each with pools between — the little trickle falls, animated, with foam where each step lands.

The culvert: Boulder Park Dr does not bridge the creek. The road runs continuous at grade over a big round storm drain — 2.5 m (8.2 ft) across, corrugated galvanised steel, 19 m long — passing through the ground under the road, its round mouths open on both embankment faces with the creek flowing through. The road rides a low causeway (about 2.6 m of fill at the creek, 0.5 m of cover over the pipe) — what a real big-pipe crossing looks like in this shallow swale. No deck, no rails, no gap.

The trees are real trees — 478 of them, placed one by one from the package canopy data, same as the crest scene: mature hardwoods 12–20 m, foliage starting about 8 ft up, just over the 6′1″ player's head, trunks running up into the crowns.

Canopy-off-open-water — checked against the actual tree list: 0 trees stand in or over Tatum Lake or any open water in this scene (the inspection found the canopy data roofing the lake — here the trees obey the water). Shore trees stand on the bank; none stands in the road corridor or the falls channel either.
