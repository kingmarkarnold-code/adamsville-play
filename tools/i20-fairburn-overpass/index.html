I-20 × Fairburn Rd — the overpass TEST▾

A test scene, same treatment as the crest and Tatum ones — package terrain, real individual trees, look-test roads — framed on the crossing where Fairburn Rd passes OVER I-20 on a deck. This is the crossing that failed in the old map: vehicles floated and there was no bridge. Test scene only, not game data; no package data was changed.

The ground first (it was audited for this scene): under the span, the package ground is the freeway's own bench — 864 ft at the crossing — falling steadily from the west and levelling east. Unfilled, unraised: the scene's only touch there seats the two carriageways on their own natural profile (largest change under the span: 5.8 ft of levelling, and raised nowhere by more than 0.4 ft).

The bridge is a structure built TO that ground: a deck 70 m (230 ft) long spanning the whole freeway, girders under the slab, parapets on both sides, open air beneath — clearance 16 ft (4.9 m) over the I-20 surface. Fill exists only at the ends: the north approach rides an embankment (up to about 19 ft of fill) down to the deck, and on the south Fairburn simply steps off its high ground — its approaches stand at 880 ft (south) and 869 ft (north) against the freeway's 864.

One honest note: the archived road lines near this crossing disagree with each other — the freeway's recorded lines bunch on one side and overlap. So the scene seats the freeway in the ground's own bench (the flat the terrain itself makes for it), which is the referee — the lines get redrawn from the ground at the roads step, not the other way round.

The trees are real trees — 715 of them, placed one by one from the package canopy data, same as the crest scene: mature hardwoods 12–20 m, foliage starting about 8 ft up, trunks running up into the crowns. None stands in either road corridor or under the deck.

What to check: from the driver's view on I-20, the deck is overhead with open span beneath and the ground runs continuous under it. From the side, the fill is at the two ends only — nothing is heaped across the freeway, and nothing floats.
