Tatum Lake — shoreline check▾

Tatum Lake, up close — drawn from the built terrain test package itself (10 m grid, full detail). The pale line is Boulder Park Dr for orientation; the dark green masses are tree canopy. This is the shoreline inspection: is the water properly surrounded by land? Vertical scale is TRUE (1.0) unless you change it below.

What the audit found: the land stands above the water all the way around — the only place it falls away is the outlet on the south shore, where the creek leaves. At the meeting point the surface steps down 6.0 ft — the little falls you described, a touch bigger than "a foot or a few"; noted for the structures pass. The lake bed is flat at 820½ ft under the water — no dry humps poking through.

One defect — in the tree layer, not the ground: the canopy source (NLCD) misreads this small wooded lake as forest (81% cover), so as stored, the tree masses roof the water. The ground and water underneath are right. Use Canopy: OFF in Controls to inspect the shoreline; the zone build must keep canopy off open water — the same clearing rule as houses.
