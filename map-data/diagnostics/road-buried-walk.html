King James Drive Southwest — the street runs under the hill

This quarter mile of street is stored as one straight line with no points in the middle, but a hill stands between its ends — so the street cuts through the hill and runs up to 15 m (about 50 ft) underground at the crest (red marker). The dark ribbon is the street at its true height and width; where it vanishes, it's under the ground. The cyan line (X-ray) shows its buried path.

Walk him: joystick (bottom left) or W A S D / arrow keys. He walks the real surface — on the street he follows the street, even underground. The camera follows; drag to look around. The readout at the top tells you when he's under the ground and by how much.
